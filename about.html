Our Story

Born from a Passion for Design

MPS Studio was founded by design enthusiasts who believed that beautiful, strategic design shouldn't be a luxury reserved for big corporations.

"We started with a single client, a shared laptop, and an unshakeable belief that design can transform any business."

With 20+ projects delivered for happy clients, we've grown into a full-service creative studio working with startups, established brands, and everything in between. Our team brings expertise across brand identity, UI/UX, motion, and print — all under one roof.

Our Mission
"To make world-class design accessible to every business, and to use our craft to tell stories that matter."

We believe every business has a unique story worth telling — and that the right visual language can transform how the world perceives you. Our mission is to be the creative partner that helps you tell that story with power, clarity, and beauty.
