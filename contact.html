Common Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Here are answers to the questions clients ask us most before initiating a project.

How fast do you deliver initial concepts?

For branding and logo design, we present our first exploratory creative directions within 3 to 5 business days. Full brand systems and digital websites typically take 2 to 3 weeks.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes! Over 40% of our clients are outside India (including the US, UK, UAE, and Singapore). We communicate asynchronously via email, Loom video walkthroughs, and scheduled video calls.

What are your payment terms?

We work with an industry-standard 50% milestone advance to initiate project work, and the remaining 50% upon final delivery and sign-off. We accept UPI, NEFT, Net Banking, and Cards.

What source files do I receive?

You receive 100% intellectual property ownership upon completion, including raw vector files (.AI, .EPS, .SVG), Figma UI kits, production-ready exports, and complete brand style guidelines.
